# CommonsHome
**Where homes learn how to sell themselves.**
- HTML Mirror:  [https://roxanneardary.com/commonshome-specification/](https://roxanneardary.com/commonshome-specification/)  

---

CommonsHome is an open-source, modular AI platform for home staging, real estate marketing, spatial intelligence, and listing performance optimization.

Built under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, CommonsHome combines computer vision, generative AI, spatial reconstruction, buyer psychology modeling, and real-world analytics into a unified real estate intelligence system.

The platform is designed to help homeowners, real estate agents, photographers, designers, investors, and developers transform ordinary property photos into fully optimized, market-ready listings.

---

# Core Philosophy

CommonsHome is built around the idea that real estate presentation should be:

- Open and transparent
- Community-driven
- Modular and extensible
- AI-assisted but human-guided
- Continuously improving through real-world feedback

CommonsHome treats every property as a living spatial and market system where layouts, staging, photography, marketing, and buyer engagement all influence one another.

---

# System Workflow

```text
Upload → Analyze → Stage → Optimize → Market → Validate → Learn
```

The platform continuously improves recommendations using real listing performance data and market feedback loops.
