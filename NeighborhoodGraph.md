# NeighborhoodGraph

### Where Places Become Interactive.

NeighborhoodGraph is an AGPL-3.0+ open-source platform for housing intelligence, ownership transparency, digital twins, infrastructure analysis, community memory preservation, cooperative housing management, and AI-powered community intelligence.

The platform combines property intelligence, GIS mapping, ownership research, infrastructure visualization, digital twin technology, citizen reporting, historical preservation, and artificial intelligence into a unified community knowledge ecosystem.

---

# Vision

NeighborhoodGraph seeks to create an open, transparent, and community-focused platform that helps citizens, researchers, planners, housing advocates, municipalities, and developers better understand the places where people live.

The platform is designed to transform disconnected public records, maps, imagery, infrastructure data, and community knowledge into interactive experiences and actionable insights.
