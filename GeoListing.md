# GeoListing

### Open Semantic Infrastructure for Real Estate

GeoListing is an AGPL 3.0+ licensed modular AI platform for real estate marketing, listing intelligence, compliance automation, SEO optimization, semantic search enrichment, and LLM-ready property discovery.

Built for brokerages, agents, developers, MLS ecosystems, proptech platforms, and self-hosted deployments, GeoListing combines semantic search optimization, real estate advertising compliance, AI-powered content generation, market intelligence, legal intelligence, and advanced publishing infrastructure into a unified open platform.

GeoListing is designed for the next generation of real estate discovery by optimizing listings for:

- Search engines
- AI assistants
- Semantic retrieval systems
- Vector search
- Voice search
- Knowledge graphs
- Large language models
- Local search ecosystems
