## Overview

MortaLens is a comprehensive, AI-powered mortgage intelligence, short sale evaluation, and loan resolution management platform designed to modernize how mortgage hardship cases, short sale applications, and mortgage workout opportunities are evaluated, processed, and resolved. The platform provides advanced financial analysis, hardship verification, fraud detection, credit impact assessment, loan eligibility evaluation, document intelligence, and transaction management. Its modular architecture allows it to serve banks, mortgage lenders, loan servicers, credit unions, mortgage investors, mortgage insurance companies, independent short sale processing firms, real estate brokerages, attorneys, title companies, and property owners.

MortaLens is designed as a universal platform that can accommodate organizations of different sizes, business models, operational requirements, and regulatory environments. Organizations can configure their own workflows, approval authorities, investor requirements, document standards, and access permissions without requiring separate software implementations. The platform supports enterprise deployments, independent organizational deployments, multi-tenant environments, and white-label implementations. Its core intelligence engine provides a common foundation for financial analysis, mortgage eligibility evaluation, and resolution recommendations across participating organizations.

The platform incorporates an owner-facing preliminary assessment portal that allows property owners to evaluate their financial circumstances, mortgage obligations, hardship conditions, credit profile, and potential short sale eligibility before committing to a formal application. Authorized professionals can use the same underlying intelligence to conduct comprehensive financial reviews, investigate discrepancies, evaluate applicable loan guidelines, prepare documentation, and generate approval or denial recommendations. Human oversight is mandatory for final decisions, with complete evidence tracking, reviewer authorization, and auditability throughout the process.

MortaLens is designed to extend beyond traditional short sale processing into a comprehensive mortgage loss mitigation and resolution intelligence platform. It evaluates alternative mortgage resolution options, including loan modifications, repayment plans, forbearance, reinstatement, deeds in lieu of foreclosure, and other applicable solutions. Its extensible architecture allows organizations to integrate financial institutions, credit bureaus, property valuation services, mortgage servicing platforms, electronic signature providers, title companies, and other specialized services through optional plugin modules.

## Core Objectives

- Automate the collection, organization, extraction, and analysis of mortgage and financial documentation.
- Evaluate whether borrowers demonstrate legitimate financial hardship under applicable mortgage and investor guidelines.
- Analyze bank statements and financial records to establish an accurate picture of a borrower's financial circumstances.
- Identify financial discrepancies, unexplained transactions, undisclosed assets, and potential fraud indicators.
- Evaluate property values, outstanding mortgage balances, liens, estimated sale proceeds, and transaction feasibility.
- Determine potential short sale eligibility under applicable loan programs, investor requirements, and mortgage insurance guidelines.
- Provide property owners with preliminary eligibility and financial assessments before formal application submission.
- Analyze potential credit consequences associated with short sales and alternative mortgage resolution options.
- Automatically identify, generate, compile, and route required application and transaction documents for signature.
- Generate evidence-based approval, conditional approval, denial, and further investigation recommendations.
- Require qualified human review and authorization before final approval or denial.
- Maintain comprehensive audit trails documenting financial analysis, guideline evaluations, reviewer actions, and final decisions.
- Support multiple organizations, configurable business processes, and independent deployments.
- Provide an expandable foundation for additional mortgage loss mitigation and financial resolution services.

## Artificial Intelligence and Decision Intelligence Requirements

MortaLens must provide explainable, evidence-based AI analysis across all applicable modules.

- Every material financial finding must reference its supporting source documentation.
- Extracted financial information must be distinguishable from borrower-reported information and AI-generated estimates.
- Financial calculations must be reproducible and independently reviewable.
- AI-generated recommendations must identify the applicable eligibility rules and guideline versions.
- The system must disclose missing information and uncertainty.
- AI confidence scores must not be treated as proof of eligibility, fraud, or financial hardship.
- Financial discrepancies must be presented with sufficient context for human investigation.
- AI-generated documents must be reviewed before execution where required.
- Model updates must be versioned and subject to validation.
- Historical decisions must retain the model and guideline information applicable at the time of review.
- AI outputs must be monitored for accuracy, consistency, and discriminatory outcomes.
- The platform must support human correction and feedback.
- Material AI errors must be identifiable, traceable, and correctable.
- No AI recommendation may bypass required human authorization.
