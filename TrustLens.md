# TrustLens

### Prepared Listings, Happy Buyers

**TrustLens** is an open-source property transparency platform designed to help **home sellers and property owners prepare fully transparent listings** before bringing a property to market.

Using AI-assisted analysis and location-aware legal guidance, TrustLens helps sellers identify and gather all documents, permits, disclosures, and inspections that buyers will expect during the transaction process.

TrustLens is part of the **OpenProperty** ecosystem. https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/OpenProperty

---

# Mission

Many real estate transactions encounter delays, disputes, or failed negotiations because important property information was missing, incomplete, or discovered late in the process.

TrustLens exists to:

* help sellers prepare fully transparent property listings
* anticipate buyer questions and documentation needs
* verify permits and renovation compliance
* guide sellers through state-required disclosure requirements
* ensure that properties are presented with complete and accurate information

TrustLens helps create **ethical and prepared real estate transactions from the beginning.**

---

# Key Features

## AI Disclosure Preparation

TrustLens guides sellers through the creation of complete disclosure packages.

The system helps identify:

* required property disclosures
* common defect categories
* renovation documentation
* maintenance history
* environmental disclosures
* known property conditions

This ensures sellers provide information **buyers are legally and practically expecting.**

---

## State-Law-Aware Guidance

TrustLens automatically determines the location of the property and evaluates requirements based on:

* state disclosure laws
* county regulations
* township permit requirements
* zoning rules
* homeowner association requirements

All recommendations prioritize **legal compliance for the property’s jurisdiction.**

---

## Permit and Renovation Verification

TrustLens helps sellers verify renovations such as:

* additions
* sunrooms
* finished basements
* electrical upgrades
* plumbing modifications
* decks
* structural changes

The platform identifies:

* permits that should exist
* inspections that should have occurred
* documentation buyers may request

---

## Inspection Preparation

TrustLens recommends proactive inspections that sellers may want to obtain before listing a property.

Examples include:

* structural inspection
* electrical inspection
* plumbing inspection
* HVAC inspection
* roof inspection
* pest inspection

Providing inspection documentation in advance can improve buyer confidence and reduce negotiation surprises.

---

## Buyer Question Anticipation

TrustLens predicts the questions buyers will likely ask during the due diligence phase.

The system generates recommended preparation items for:

* permit history
* repair records
* contractor documentation
* utility information
* HOA documentation
* township compliance

This helps sellers prepare answers **before the questions arise.**

---

## Township and Compliance Requests

TrustLens provides guidance for requesting important documents from local governments.

Examples include:

* certificate of occupancy records
* permit history reports
* zoning confirmations
* property tax records
* environmental notices

This ensures sellers can provide documentation directly from official sources.

---

## Transparency Passport

TrustLens allows sellers to generate a **Transparency Passport**, a shareable report summarizing property readiness.

The report may include:

* disclosure completeness
* permit verification
* inspection documentation
* renovation records
* compliance confirmations

This report helps buyers review property information with confidence.

---

# Document Upload and Analysis

Sellers can upload property documents including:

* disclosure forms
* permits
* contractor invoices
* inspection reports
* HOA documents
* zoning confirmations

TrustLens analyzes documents to identify missing items or potential buyer concerns.
