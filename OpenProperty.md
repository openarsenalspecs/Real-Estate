# OpenProperty
### Transparency for Every Home

**OpenProperty** is an open-source real estate transparency platform designed to bring clarity, ethics, and accountability to property transactions.  

The platform empowers **buyers, renters, and sellers** by using AI-driven analysis, legal compliance awareness, and public records verification to ensure that every property transaction is informed, transparent, and fair.

OpenProperty combines two primary applications:

- **HouseLens** — Buyer and renter protection  https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/HouseLens
- **TrustLens** — Seller transparency and preparation  https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/TrustLens

Together they form a unified ecosystem that helps uncover risks, verify disclosures, and prepare complete property records.

---

# Mission

Real estate transactions are among the largest financial decisions people make, yet property information is often incomplete, inconsistent, or difficult to verify.

OpenProperty exists to:

- increase transparency in property transactions
- protect buyers and renters from hidden risks
- help sellers provide complete and trustworthy listings
- provide tools that verify disclosures, permits, inspections, and compliance
- create an open global knowledge base for property due diligence

# Ecosystem Overview

## HouseLens
**From Unknowns to Insights**

HouseLens helps **buyers and renters evaluate properties** before committing to a purchase or lease.

Features include:

- disclosure document analysis
- township and permit verification
- inspection recommendations by licensed professionals
- property risk scoring
- insurance risk detection
- renovation legality checks
- negotiation intelligence
- repair cost estimation
- rental compliance scanning
- fraud and scam detection
- HOA and condo risk analysis
- neighborhood intelligence

HouseLens ensures that **no property question goes unasked and no risk goes unseen.**

---

## TrustLens
**Prepared Listings, Happy Buyers**

TrustLens helps **property owners and sellers prepare transparent listings** before bringing a property to market.

Features include:

- AI-guided disclosure preparation
- permit verification
- inspection planning
- contractor license verification
- township compliance document requests
- buyer question anticipation
- listing transparency scoring
- secure document vault for property records
- transparency reports for potential buyers

TrustLens helps sellers create **complete, credible listings that build buyer trust.**

# Core Platform Principles

## State-Law-First Analysis

Every property evaluation begins with **location awareness**.

The system determines:

1. state property disclosure laws
2. county regulations
3. township permit requirements
4. local zoning restrictions
5. HOA or condominium regulations

All AI recommendations are generated based on these legal frameworks.

---

## Evidence-Based AI

Every recommendation is supported by verifiable sources:

- public permit records
- disclosure requirements
- local building codes
- inspection standards
- insurance risk guidelines

The platform prioritizes **traceable information over assumptions**.

---

# Property Transparency ID

OpenProperty introduces a **Property Transparency ID** system.

Each property can receive a permanent transparency record containing:

- permit history
- inspection records
- disclosure documents
- renovation documentation
- compliance verification

This functions similarly to a **vehicle history report, but for real estate.**

---

# Transparency Passport

Sellers can generate a **Transparency Passport** that summarizes:

- permit verification
- inspection reports
- disclosure completeness
- compliance status
- transparency score

This report can be shared with buyers to improve confidence in a listing.
